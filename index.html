About Pilaniya Farms

Pilaniya Farms is a family-owned farm dedicated to producing high-quality, natural food products. We use traditional methods and sustainable practices to nurture the soil and grow flavorful, nutrient-dense crops.

Our mission is simple: deliver honest, transparent products you can trust — premium peanuts, stone-ground wheat flour, and whole spices — carefully processed and packed for freshness.
