About Pilaniya Farms

Pilaniya Farms is a small family-run farm committed to regenerative agriculture, soil health, and delivering the freshest produce possible. We focus on seasonal crops and partner with local markets and restaurants.

Products

Seasonal Vegetables

Leafy greens, root vegetables, and market-ready produce harvested at peak freshness.

Fresh Fruits

Small-batch fruit varieties grown without synthetic pesticides.

CSA Boxes

Weekly or biweekly boxes packed with a curated selection from the farm.

Sustainability

We use cover cropping, composting, and reduced-till practices to improve soil health and biodiversity. Our goal is to leave the land healthier than we found it.
